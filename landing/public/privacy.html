Privacy

This website hosts a public No meme collection and a private personal project dashboard.

Private sign-in

Google sign-in verifies your Google account identifier and email address. The website does not receive your Google password or request access to your Gmail, Drive, contacts, or files. Google handles account sign-in and recovery.

The private dashboard uses secure session cookies. Session records containing your account identifier, email, expiry, and a hashed session token are stored privately using Cloudflare. Sessions expire after 12 hours. Logging out clears this browser's cookies and requests deletion of its session record; storage propagation may take a short time.

Public memes

Memes added by the owner are public. Visitors can view and save them. The website remembers the last selected meme in this browser tab so the next selection can be different.

Hosting

Cloudflare serves this site and stores uploaded memes and private session records. Google and Cloudflare also process requests under their own privacy policies. The separate party website has its own host access and photo storage.

This site does not sell your personal information. For questions about this personal project or removal of content, contact IDontThinkIWill Support.
